# Editorial release scorecard · 100 points

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit expectation |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem contract and source fidelity | 10 | Official title, difficulty, API, constraints and semantics verified |
| Algorithm selection and explanation | 15 | Preconditions and why the pattern applies are explicit |
| Invariant, recurrence and proof | 20 | Initialization/base, maintenance, completeness and termination are sound |
| Complexity and implementation | 15 | Complete correct code; time, auxiliary/output space and mutation audited |
| SVG and visual trace | 15 | Three distinct, accurate, readable, standalone diagrams |
| Test quality | 10 | Boundary, adversarial and differential/reference tests |
| Narrative and accessibility | 5 | Clear progression, semantic HTML and keyboard-operable controls |
| Browser, print and offline QA | 5 | Required viewports, no overflow, broken assets or console errors |
| Evidence and publication hygiene | 5 | Evidence ledger, unique metadata, synced mirrors and release traceability |

Release gate: at least 90/100 and no critical correctness, attribution, security or accessibility defect. A score is editorial judgment, not automated proof. Automated checks must report measured facts only. A failed hard gate cannot be offset by visual polish.
